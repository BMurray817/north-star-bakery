Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood-focused bakery built around the idea that fresh food brings people together. We welcome commuters, families, and neighbors looking for a daily treat or a special-order cake.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We aim to choose quality ingredients carefully, reduce unnecessary waste, and consider local suppliers when possible. Ask our team about ingredients and allergens before ordering.
